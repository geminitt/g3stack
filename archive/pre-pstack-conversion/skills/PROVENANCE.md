# Sources, licenses and adaptations

The g3-* entries are maintained g3stack adaptations, not original vendor plugins
or an endorsement by their authors. They retain selected methods, consolidate
local Research/recovery guidance and replace host-specific workflow assumptions.
They are not unchanged copies or claims of original authorship of those methods.

[provenance.json](provenance.json) records exact per-skill upstream files, pinned
URLs and SHA-256 values, plus migrated local-source identities. It is attribution,
not a second capability catalog. Original local content is retained in the
skill-library migration recovery archive before its old entry is retired.

All notices and resources remain in their original canonical vendor locations.
Distribute the complete checkout with those files; these are not independent
single-folder skill distributions. Resource links, third-party media references
and optional services do not supply host execution support or service access.

Local changes include conditional task discovery, one project controller,
project-native state/harness reuse, practical regression selection, permission-
bounded native delegation and model use, early premise recovery, independent
assessment and honest target/coverage limits. Specialist guides are read only
when a task needs their deeper resources; their parent orchestrators and plugins
are not activated. Matt Pocock sources are not included in this library.

Coordination design influences are recorded separately from the 16 skill source
mappings in provenance.json. Figure It Out, poteto-mode's workflow selection and
Autonomous Run informed goal ownership and evidence-driven continuation. The
approved g3stack contract retains its own controls and imports no vendor runtime,
model choices, mandatory delegation, delivery actions or background wake mechanism.
